# Nepa Keyboard privacy policy

Effective date: 2026-08-09

Nepa Keyboard is an offline Android input method. It does not request Android's
internet permission and does not transmit typed text, suggestions, settings, or
usage information to Ampixa or to third parties.

## Information processed on the device

Nepa Keyboard processes key presses and nearby editor text on the device to
provide typing, deletion, composition, transliteration, script conversion, and
dictionary suggestions. Suggestions are disabled for password and other private
input fields. This text is not sent off the device by Nepa Keyboard.

The app stores keyboard preferences on the device, including enabled languages,
the active language, theme, keyboard height, feedback options, suggestion
preference, and recent emoji. The setup typing field is not saved and is
excluded from autofill. Android backup is disabled for the app.

## Network access and third parties

Nepa Keyboard does not include the Android internet permission. Its language
resources, layouts, transliteration maps, conversions, emoji data, and
suggestion dictionaries are bundled with the app and run locally. The app does
not include advertising or analytics SDKs.

The Licenses and attributions screen can ask another installed app, such as a
web browser, to open a source or license link. Nepa Keyboard itself does not
make that network request.

## Data sharing, retention, and deletion

Nepa Keyboard does not collect or share personal data with Ampixa. On-device
preferences and recent emoji remain until the user changes them, clears the
app's storage, or uninstalls the app. Users can delete all app-held information
through Android Settings by clearing Nepa Keyboard's storage or uninstalling it.

## Children

Nepa Keyboard does not knowingly collect personal information from children or
from any other user.

## Changes and contact

Material changes to this policy will be reflected by updating its effective
date. Privacy questions can be sent to the support contact shown on the app's
Google Play listing.

Before Google Play submission, publish this policy at a stable public HTTPS URL
